The Kowa Youth Entertainment Association (KYEA) exists to unite young people through entertainment, culture, creativity, and talent development. It provides a positive platform for members to showcase their ability, promote peace and unity, preserve Kowa's cultural heritage, and contribute to the social and community development of Kowa Chiefdom.
